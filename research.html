Research / 研究

My research interests are in the area of secure and reliable Internet from Space.

1. Optical Wireless and Non-Terrestrial Networks

  • Space-air-ground-sea integrated networks (satellite, HAP, UAV, maritime)
  • FSO/RF and FSO/sub-THz hybrid communications
  • Optical intelligent reflecting surfaces (IRS) and relaying

2. Quantum AI for Secure and Intelligent Communications

  • Quantum key distribution (QKD) and entanglement-based links
  • Quantum machine learning and quantum-enhanced DRL
  • Federated/quantum DRL for resource and network optimization

3. AI-Driven Sensing and ISAC

  • Sensing-assisted beam tracking and predictive control
  • Multi-agent DRL for deployment, resource allocation and handover
  • Integrated sensing, communications and computing

Research / 研究

My research interests are in the area of secure and reliable Internet from Space.

Optical Wireless Networks for Internet from Space

  • Space-air-ground integrated networks (satellite, HAP, UAV)
  • Mixed FSO/RF and hybrid FSO/sub-THz systems
  • Network protocol design and cross-layer optimization

Quantum Key Distribution

  • Satellite- and UAV-based FSO/QKD systems
  • Key reconciliation and error estimation
  • Entanglement-based QKD and satellite selection

AI/ML for Non-Terrestrial Networks

  • Multi-agent deep reinforcement learning for UAV deployment
  • Quantum-inspired and LLM-assisted DRL
  • Joint placement, handover and power allocation

Intelligent Reflecting Surfaces and Relaying

  • Optical IRS-aided aerial networks
  • Integrated access and backhaul
  • Channel modeling under turbulence and clouds

Integrated Sensing and Communications

  • Sensing-aided FSO beam tracking
  • Sensing, communications and computing for optical wireless networks
  • Sensing-assisted QKD for secure IoV

Error Control and Reliability

  • HARQ, LDPC and network coding designs
  • Channel prediction-driven adaptation
  • Link adaptation over optical satellite links

Bio

Hoang Le is currently an Associate Professor with the School of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Aizu (UoA), Japan. His research interests are in the areas of optical wireless communications, satellite/vehicular networks, applied AI/ML, and network security-based quantum cryptography. He was a recipient of several awards, including the 2018 KICS/IEEE ICTC Best Paper Award, 2019 IEEE VTS Tokyo Chapter Young Researcher Encouragement Award, 2019 IEEE ComSoc Sendai Chapter Student Excellent Research Award, and the honor of being a selected young researcher for the 9th Heidelberg Laureate Forum in 2022. Other accolades include the 2022 IEEE ICCE Best Paper Award, 2023 ICETC Best Poster Award, 2024 IEICE Comex Top Downloaded Letter, and 2024 IEEE/IET CSNDSP Best Paper Award. Dr. Hoang Le is currently the president of SAISUA at the University of Aizu. He has also served as Chair of the IEEE University of Aizu Student Branch and as a committee member of the IEEE Sendai Young Professionals Affinity Group. He is a member of IEEE and IEICE.
